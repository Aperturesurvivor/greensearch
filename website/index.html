⚡ How it works

Every message runs through three steps before answering:

  • PromptShrink — strips filler from your question (watch the preview as you type)
  • Session cache — reuses a previous answer if you asked before
  • Routing — picks the shortest sufficient answer length
Routing thresholds

🌱 Sprout ≤12 words → ~2 sentences
🌿 Canopy 13–34 words → ~4 sentences
🌲 Forest 35+ words, complexity marker, or 2+ questions → ~6 sentences

🌍 Finland & carbon

Designed to run on nearly carbon-free electricity. Reference: Google Cloud Finland — 98% carbon-free, 30 gCO₂eq/kWh.

Note: this demo runs in your browser, not in Finland. Savings shown come from fewer tokens only. Clean-energy hosting is a separate lever for a live version.

How numbers are calculated

Tokens: words × 1.33, min 1.
CO₂e: tokens × 0.0018 g.
Baseline: original question + Forest answer, no trimming or routing.
GreenSearch: shrunk question + routed answer (0 on cache hit).
Published per-prompt figures for real models differ by 10× or more.

Honest limits

No live model. Answers are pre-written for 6 topics. Anything else gets an honest fallback, not a made-up answer.
