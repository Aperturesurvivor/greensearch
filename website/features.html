Arboris

How Arboris works

Every message runs through three steps before it is answered.

1. PromptShrink

Removes filler words from your question before it reaches the model.

2. Session cache

Repeats a previous answer when you ask the same thing twice in a session. No model call, no new tokens.

3. Right-sized answers

Picks the shortest sufficient answer length by question length: Sprout (12 words or fewer), Canopy, or Forest (35+ words, or a complexity phrase).

Energy numbers

Each answer shows how much CO2 it prevented versus a typical ChatGPT query.

  • Token counts come from the provider. The typing preview estimates words x 1.33.
  • Energy is output tokens x 0.0006 Wh x 400 g CO2e/kWh. Input tokens count 1/800 as much.
